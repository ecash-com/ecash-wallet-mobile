One wallet, every network. A wallet is now your recovery phrase, and you switch it between eCash, Bitcoin and L2L Signet from the network chip on the Home screen. Creating or importing a wallet no longer asks you to pick a network. If you held the same phrase as separate wallets on different networks, they are merged into one wallet the first time you open this version. Nothing needs to be re-entered, and your recovery phrases are untouched.

Also in this release:
• Thunder mainnet is in the network switcher, nested under eCash.
• Coins you receive can be spent before they confirm. Send prefers confirmed coins, the review shows the exact fee, and it warns when a payment depends on a coin that hasn't confirmed yet. Home shows how much of your balance is still unconfirmed.
• Creating a wallet: the words appear on the grid as you draw, then you name the wallet.
• The ECX price now comes from eCash.com, under eCash balances and on the Dashboard, with its 24-hour change.
• Retired test networks (Alphanet, Betanet, Thunder Betanet) are hidden by default. A Settings toggle brings them back; nothing is deleted.
• One Confirm for every send. The extra "Send real eCash?" alert is gone; the review and your device unlock are the gate on every network.
• Fixed: switching networks did not always refresh the balance on the network you switched to.
• Fixed: "Deposit to one of my wallets" was missing from the Thunder deposit screen. It now lists your wallets, with the one you are in first.
