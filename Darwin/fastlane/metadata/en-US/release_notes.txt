Moved to a new phone? Your recovery phrase never leaves the phone it was saved on, so a wallet restored from a backup arrives with its balance and history but without its phrase. Until now the app gave no sign of that, and the wallet could not send.

In this version:
• The app tells you when a wallet is missing its recovery phrase: on Home, on Receive, in Settings and in the wallet list.
• You can enter the phrase (or the private key, for a single-key wallet) to restore the same wallet, with its name and history, instead of importing it again. The app accepts it only if it belongs to that wallet.
• Send and Withdraw take you to the restore step until the phrase is back.
• The backup screen now says up front that the phrase stays on this phone and will not move to a new one. Write it down before you switch phones.
