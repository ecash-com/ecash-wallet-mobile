Most wallets ask you to trust that your phone picked a good random number. eCash.com Wallet lets you generate it yourself.

Swipe across the entropy pad, and your own motion — which characters you cross, how quickly, how long you linger — becomes the input your recovery phrase is derived from. It is deterministic and auditable: the same input always produces the same words, the exact string being hashed is shown on screen, and you can reproduce the whole derivation yourself with a single SHA-256 command. Your input is mixed with the system's own randomness, so the result is never weaker than the standard path — only harder to predict.

This is a self-custodial wallet for eCash, the Bitcoin hardfork from Layer Two Labs that brings Drivechain to Bitcoin. Your keys never leave your device. Wallets are generated on-device, recovery phrases are held in the Android Keystore and excluded from device backups, and every transaction is signed locally with the Bitcoin Dev Kit (BDK).


MANY WALLETS, MANY NETWORKS

• Hold as many wallets as you like — each with its own separate recovery phrase — and switch between them instantly
• Bitcoin and eCash side by side, every wallet carrying a coloured network badge so the two can never be confused
• Create Native SegWit or Taproot wallets
• Restore any 12- or 24-word BIP39 recovery phrase, including legacy and nested SegWit accounts, custom derivation paths, and single WIF private keys


SEND AND RECEIVE

• QR codes and BIP21 payment links, with a built-in scanner
• Choose your fee before you send
• Review recipient, amount, fee, and network on one screen before anything is signed
• Send to another of your own wallets without copying an address around
• Transaction history with confirmations, fees, replace-by-fee status, and block-explorer links
• Split coins — separate your forked eCash from the Bitcoin sharing the same coin, in a single transaction


SECURITY

• Your fingerprint, face, or device passcode on launch, and again before every payment
• Your private key is loaded for exactly one signing operation and then dropped — balances, addresses, and history all run watch-only
• Balances are covered in the recents screen
• Point any network at your own Electrum or Esplora server
• Free and open source under the GPL


YOUR COINS, YOUR CONTROL

This is a non-custodial wallet. We never hold your keys, your recovery phrase, or your funds — and we cannot recover them for you. Write your recovery phrase down and keep it somewhere safe.
