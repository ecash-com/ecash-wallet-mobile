You now generate your own randomness. Every new wallet starts at the entropy pad: swipe across it and your own motion becomes the input your recovery phrase is derived from — deterministic, shown on screen, and verifiable yourself.

Also in this release:
• New wallets default to eCash
• Create Native SegWit or Taproot wallets (legacy can still be imported)
• Watch your words change as you swipe, and review them before the wallet is created
